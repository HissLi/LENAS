## Notes

- Metric definitions are identical to `Unet/evaluate.py`: D1/D95/D99 for targets, D_0.1cc/Dmean for OARs; the DVH score is the mean absolute difference over all structure-metric entries.
- The predicted and GT doses are compared on the same structure masks from the ground-truth NIfTI files; TTA was enabled during inference.
